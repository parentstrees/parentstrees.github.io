Planting Christmas Trees

The best soil is that of sandy loam. Full sun to partial shade is also best for Christmas trees of all kinds.

There is a lot to think about when planting Christmas trees, such as soil ph, density, sun vs shade areas, and what size to plant.

Transplants come in many sizes in bare root, seedling, or potted form. The soil should have a low ph of 5.6 to 6.2 for planting conifers.

I haven't determined what the best size transplant to plant as of yet. I've tried mostly 2-3yr transplants.
